Flashlight

The 0–100% control maps to a maximum 25% electrical PWM duty pending physical validation. GPIO4 uses a separate PWM timer from the camera and SD runs in 1-bit mode. No continuous full-power or thermal safety claim is made.

Recording schedule

Schedules pause until the clock is synchronized. Overnight windows belong to the day they start. The saved timezone offset must be refreshed after daylight-saving changes.
