# Robetix Vision — everyday use

## Without Internet

1. Power on the camera.
2. Join its **ROBETIX-VISION-XX** Wi-Fi using the private password supplied with it.
3. Open **http://192.168.4.1**.

Home opens without another password or code. Watch the camera, use the flashlight,
take photos, record to a working memory card, and manage saved files. Local use
does not require an online account.

## With Internet

Open **https://vision.robetix.com**. A trusted browser restores your existing
account session. Choose your camera to watch and control it.

For the first online connection, use Camera setup in the local app to save your
home Wi-Fi. Sign in on the online app, choose Add Camera, and follow the pairing
instructions. Keep the local setup page open while connecting the camera; switch
your phone to home Wi-Fi or mobile data to use the online app. Camera Wi-Fi itself
does not provide Internet.

A ready, paired camera can offer automatic online viewing when this browser can
also reach the online service. Choose **Keep using locally** to stay. Recording,
setup and other active local operations prevent automatic switching. If the
Internet is unavailable, local viewing keeps working. The online app cannot
change your phone's Wi-Fi automatically.

## Reset

While connected to camera Wi-Fi, open Settings → Factory Reset → Confirm.
The camera stops recording safely, clears settings and restarts. Firmware and
memory-card files are kept. Reconnect using its original private factory Wi-Fi
label. Cloud account ownership remains protected; an old registration may need
to be disconnected by its existing owner before pairing again.

Keep the supplied private label safe. If access is lost, use the separate physical
USB maintenance tool supplied by your installer. It requires possession of the
camera and a supported computer browser; there is no recovery button in the
ordinary camera connection screen.
