# Robetix Vision — everyday use

## Open your cameras

Open **https://vision.robetix.com**. Your trusted browser restores your account
session. Select a camera to watch, record, take photos, use the flashlight and
open Saved, Activity or Settings. Files are kept on that camera's memory card.
A camera must have power and an Internet connection for remote controls.

## Without Internet

If you have opened the updated portal before, its saved offline page offers
**Open local camera**. Join the camera's **ROBETIX-VISION-XX** Wi-Fi using the
private password supplied with it, then use this button. If no saved page is
available, the offline setup address is **http://192.168.4.1**.

The same camera controls work locally without an online account or another
password. The saved portal page itself does not contain camera video. Browser
installation and Add to Home Screen availability depend on your browser.
Camera recording does not depend on an open browser or working Internet.

## Add a camera

For the first online connection, use Camera setup in the local app to save your
home Wi-Fi. Sign in on the online app, choose Add Camera, and follow the pairing
instructions. Keep the local setup page open while connecting the camera; switch
your phone to home Wi-Fi or mobile data to use the online app. Camera Wi-Fi itself
does not provide Internet.

A ready, paired camera can offer automatic online viewing when this browser can
also reach the online service. Choose **Keep using locally** to stay. Recording,
setup and other active local operations prevent automatic switching. If the
Internet is unavailable, local viewing keeps working. The online app cannot
change your phone's Wi-Fi automatically.

## Reset

While connected to camera Wi-Fi, open Settings → Factory Reset → Confirm.
The camera stops recording safely, clears settings and restarts. Firmware and
memory-card files are kept. Reconnect using its original private factory Wi-Fi
label. Cloud account ownership remains protected; an old registration may need
to be disconnected by its existing owner before pairing again.

Keep the supplied private label safe. If access is lost, use the separate physical
USB maintenance tool supplied by your installer. It requires possession of the
camera and a supported computer browser; there is no recovery button in the
ordinary camera connection screen.

## Remove a camera

Open that camera → Settings → Remove Camera. Check the camera name and confirm.
Remote access and notifications stop. Memory-card files and historical records
are kept. Removing an offline camera still revokes its cloud credential. If the
live service cannot be reached during removal, an existing video session can
remain valid for up to 90 seconds; the app explains this.
To use it online again, pair it from its private camera Wi-Fi Settings page.
